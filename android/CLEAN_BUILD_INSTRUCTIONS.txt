═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  WHY THIS HAPPENS:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When dependencies change (like we just did with Maps Compose),
Android Studio's build cache becomes outdated. Errors like:

❌ "Cannot access @Composable"
❌ "Unit is private"  
❌ "Unresolved reference"

Are often GHOST ERRORS from old cached builds, not real issues.

A clean rebuild clears the cache and rebuilds from scratch.
