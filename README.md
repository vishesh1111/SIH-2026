**Key Features Included:**

 
1. Session-Based "Start Journey" & Privacy-First Monitoring:-To ensure absolute privacy and prevent constant surveillance, safety monitoring is strictly session-based. Tourists manually initiate a "Journey." During this active session, **on-device AI** locally processes microphone audio to detect distress sounds (without ever uploading voice data to the cloud). Once the user safely reaches their destination and ends the journey, all monitoring instantly shuts down and temporary session data is cleared. 

2. Offline Emergency Mode & P2P Mesh Relay: Most safety applications fail completely in zero-connectivity zones. Our app overcomes this by utilizing a Bluetooth-based Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Relay Network to create a localized, decentralized safety net.
* Tour Group Reunification: If a tourist gets separated from their group in a dead zone (e.g., a crowded bazaar, underground transit, or remote trek), their device broadcasts an encrypted location packet. This signal "hops" across nearby devices until it reaches their team members, acting as an offline digital compass to seamlessly guide the lost member back to their group.
* Solo Traveler Hyper-Local SOS: If a solo traveler faces an emergency offline, the app can broadcast an immediate distress signal to all app users within a 200-300 meter radius. This crowd-sources immediate physical assistance from nearby verified tourists or locals long before official authorities can arrive.
* Mesh Relay to Authorities: In severe isolation, the encrypted emergency packet containing the tourist's last known GPS coordinates silently hops from device to device in the mesh. The moment it reaches any internet-enabled smartphone in the chain, it automatically relays the SOS to the authorities, while the app simultaneously guides the lost tourist to the nearest pre-downloaded offline landmark.

 3. Women’s Safety ML Engine (Predictive Routing & Anomaly Detection):- an On-Device Anomaly Detection Model utilizes edge AI to continuously analyze accelerometer data and ambient audio during an active journey. By recognising the physical patterns of a struggle (sudden drops/sprints) or acoustic distress signatures (screaming or specific trigger words), the ML model automatically deploys the SOS protocol without requiring any manual intervention from the victim. 

4. AR (Augmented Reality) Safe-Route Evasion:- When tourists are in danger or lost at night, staring down at a 2D map makes them a vulnerable target. By leveraging ARCore, the app paints a glowing virtual "Green Path" directly onto the live camera feed. This guides tourists head-up to the nearest verified Safe Haven or Police Station while actively routing them away from dynamic danger geofences. 

5. Smart Exit & 1.5km Proximity Constraint:- Designed for large reserves, parks, or crowded venues, users can select up to five potential exit gates. The system calculates the optimal route to the absolute nearest exit. To prevent guiding a panicked tourist toward distant or hazardous areas, the AI enforces a strict **1.5 km safety radius constraint**—automatically marking any gate beyond this distance as 'out-of-range'.

6. SOS Alerts: A multi-modal SOS system triggered manually, or automatically via scream detection, prolonged user inactivity, or geofence breaches. Crucially, every SOS trigger, exact coordinates, and the subsequent authority response time are logged on a Transparent Blockchain Ledger, creating an immutable public record to audit police efficiency and ensure accountability in tourist zones 

7. Early-Warning Stampede Detection:-
Famous tourist spots, festivals, and temples can sometimes get dangerously crowded. This feature acts as an early warning system to prevent tragedies. The app securely monitors how many devices are tightly packed into a specific area. If it detects extreme overcrowding or sudden, chaotic movement (like a crowd rushing), it instantly sends a "High Crowd Alert" to tourists nearby, advising them to avoid the area. At the same time, it alerts local authorities so they can manage the crowd before a stampede actually happens.

8.  AI-Powered e-FIR & Tamper-Proof Evidence Ledger:- Automatically structures natural language incident descriptions into formal First Information Reports (FIRs) with relevant BNS sections. To ensure absolute evidentiary integrity for legal proceedings, cryptographic hashes (SHA-256) of the e-FIR and all AI-analyzed multimedia evidence are instantly secured on a blockchain, proving the initial statement and evidence were never doctored. 

9. Real-Time Disaster Alerts (SACHET Integration):- Parses CAP(Common Alerting Protocol) alerts directly from the NDMA SACHET RSS feed to provide instant, location-based warnings for natural disasters (cyclones, floods, earthquakes). 

10. AI Chatbot & Real-Time Translator:- An integrated conversational agent to provide instant safety guidance, alongside a real-time translator to break down language barriers during critical situations. 

11. Emergency Contact & Social Tracking:- Manages prioritised contacts for automated emergency notifications, alongside an opt-in social map for real-time location sharing and coordination among tourist groups.
